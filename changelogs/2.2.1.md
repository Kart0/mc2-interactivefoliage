## 2.2.1 INTERACTIVE FOLIAGE - Hotfix

![2.2.1_teaser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kart0/mc2-interactivefoliage/refs/heads/yeh/media/interactive_foliage-2.2.1_teaser.gif)

---
### Added

* Polytone 'Polylights' (colored lights) support on the waving foliage (1.21.1, 26.1.2 and 26.2)
  * Works with and without Sodium

### Fixed

* VulkanMod: nearby foliage vanishing when looking in some directions (dense forests)
  * Black patches under the leaves are gone too
* Polytone shadows: waving foliage casts shadows again with the newest Polytone versions
* Crash on 26.2 with older Iris versions (1.11.2)
  * Foliage stays still while a shader pack is on; update Iris to get the waving back
* Crash on 1.20.1 Forge (Oculus) when changing a shader pack's settings
* Forge/NeoForge: foliage drawn twice (one moving, one still) in some modpacks, depending on the number of mods installed
