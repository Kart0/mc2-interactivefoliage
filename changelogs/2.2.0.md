## 2.2.0 INTERACTIVE FOLIAGE - Update

![2.2.0_teaser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kart0/mc2-interactivefoliage/refs/heads/yeh/media/interactive_foliage-2.2.0_teaser.gif)

---
### Added

* VulkanMod support (Fabric 1.21.1, 1.21.11 and 26.1.2)
  * Beryl support: foliage lit and shadowed like the terrain (same versions)
* Sable support (1.21.1): foliage on sub-levels (felled trees, ships) is drawn by the GPU renderer
  * Interaction with entities on sub-levels
  * Sable's dynamic shading
  * Works with Sodium and shader packs
  * Gravity, inertia and rebound: foliage hangs toward the real ground, lags behind a moving sub-level and bounces when it stops
  * Rain wind follows the sub-level's orientation
* cbbg (Color Banding Be Gone) compat
* Plants added by backport mods under vanilla ids now wave and interact
* LambDynamicLights is followed on every version

### Fixed

* Crash when turning a shader pack off with 'cbbg' installed
* Light and block changes now show on the foliage on the same frame
* Stutter when many light changes hit the foliage at once
* Animated foliage textures stopped moving under Sodium
* Interaction was lost without shaders when Sable (Veil) was installed
* Layered plant models flickering at the same depth on 1.21.11
